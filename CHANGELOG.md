# Changelog

How GymDay grew, from the first build on 14 November 2026 to the version sent to the stores, eleven days later.

## 1.35 (22–25 Nov 2026): ready for the stores

- **1.35.2:** 55 fixes from a real-user test, a calmer start, fewer taps in a workout, a tidier Food tab, the privacy policy and an AI permission question inside the app, 333 Indian foods with their usual servings, and the new day starting after midnight. Last night's sleep can now come from up to 10 watch screenshots or straight from the watch. Sent to the App Store for review.
- **1.35.1:** soreness worked out from your workouts, muscle by muscle, and shown on each exercise during a workout. Bigger night charts in Recovery.
- **1.35.0:** a new body map of 43 muscle parts (male and female, tap to zoom), how sore you are after each workout, and days you can swipe through in Insights.

## 1.31–1.34 (21–22 Nov 2026): insights, sleep and food you can trust

- **1.34:** 53 famous places to eat from across India, and every food in the list checked for accuracy.
- **1.33:** safe and danger zones, a switch for the watch, 50 smoothness fixes, and about 935 new dishes from India and the world.
- **1.32:** move an exercise during a workout by pressing and holding it, sleep from your watch, and day charts drawn as taller line graphs.
- **1.31:** daily insights: your last 7 or 30 days side by side, a page for every day, and a Last 7 days card on Today.

## 1.30 (21 Nov 2026): the stores and the iPhone

- Android 16 target, Report and Block in clubs, and AI reports, as Google Play and the App Store require.
- The first iPhone version: the same app in a Swift shell, with Apple Health, widgets, a Live Activity and a Share extension.

## 1.22–1.29 (19–20 Nov 2026): 21 ideas and more

- **1.22–1.24:** a new look, the Today page, search, charts you can touch and help on every page; Focus, Readiness, usual meals and Patterns; reading workouts from the watch automatically; and a speed pass that joins the page's scripts into one file, so the first screen appears about a fifth sooner.
- **1.25:** restaurant menus from the web with every size and choice, automatic progression, a morning brief and a Today widget.
- **1.26:** Settings in a new look, and meal photos with each food's nutrients worked out first.
- **1.27–1.28:** pack sizes, usual portions, photos that wait for a signal, Share to GymDay, sets per muscle, a plateau plan, an evening wrap-up, Swiggy and Zomato order screenshots, Quick Settings tiles and your year wrapped.
- **1.29:** "How was it made?" after a meal photo.

## 1.21 (18–19 Nov 2026): accessibility and many small things

- Screen readers and keyboards reach and name everything, text size follows the phone, and tap targets are bigger.
- Moving sets up and down, salt in home cooking, every abs exercise (218 exercises in all), and Sleep & heart inside Recovery.
- The AI coach chat from 1.20 was taken out again, by choice.

## 1.13–1.20 (16–17 Nov 2026): food you can count on

- The app counts every photo meal from its own food list instead of trusting the AI's numbers, with a "Check this" step when it isn't sure.
- Daily calorie needs from ICMR-NIN 2020 for ages 10 to 100, and daily limits checked against it.
- About 100 sports and activities, fresh Indian foods and drinks, caffeine, and voice logging in English, Hindi and Tamil.

## 1.0–1.12 (14–16 Nov 2026): from a workout list to a food diary

- **1.0–1.2:** your own gym days, an exercise library with photos and form tips, logging sets, gym time and timers for each exercise.
- **1.3–1.4:** accounts with an emailed code, sync between phones, clubs with friends, and heart rate and calories from Health Connect.
- **1.5–1.7:** a new look, muscle parts, calories burnt and Trainer mode.
- **1.8–1.12:** the food diary: about 21,000 foods, barcodes, recipes, meal photos read by AI, supplements, Indian packets and restaurant menus, and a Food tab with daily limits.
